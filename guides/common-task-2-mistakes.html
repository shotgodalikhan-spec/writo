10 common Task 2 mistakes and how to fix them

These are the mistakes that most often hold essays at band 6. Each one is easy to fix once you can see it.

1. Answering a different question

Writing about the general topic instead of the exact task. Fix: rewrite the question in your own words before you start.

2. No clear opinion

If the task asks for your view, give it in the introduction and repeat it in the conclusion.

3. Ideas without support

Every main idea needs an explanation and a specific example.

4. Memorised phrases

Openers like "In this day and age" or "It is a well-known fact" add nothing. Start with the topic.

5. Overusing linking words

"Firstly... Secondly... Moreover... Furthermore" in every paragraph sounds mechanical. Use them only where they help the reader.

6. Subject–verb agreement

"The number of cars are" should be "the number of cars is". Find the real subject.

7. Missing articles

"Government should invest" should be "the government should invest". Read your essay once looking only at a, an and the.

8. Informal language

Avoid contractions (don't, can't) and words like "kids", "stuff" or "a lot of".

9. Too short

Under 250 words is penalised. Plan 4–5 paragraphs and 260–300 words.

10. No time to proofread

Save the last 3–5 minutes for checking. Use the 40-minute timer in Writo to practise this habit.
