Questions

Is this an official score?

No. Writo is an independent practice tool and is not connected to the official test organisations. The band ranges are AI estimates that help you see where to improve.

How accurate is it?

Writo is in beta. We test it against essays with known examiner scores and show a range rather than a single number. Treat it as a strong second opinion, not a guarantee.

What do I get for free?

Your first check needs no sign-up. A free account gives you five checks every day and saves your results. Unlimited checks will be a paid plan later, with a 7-day refund.

Do you store my essay?

Not without an account. When you create one, your checked essays are saved to your history so you can track progress. You can switch this off at sign-up or any time later, and delete everything in one click. Read the privacy policy.

Can it check Task 1 or General Training letters?

Not yet. Writo focuses on Writing Task 2 essays so the feedback stays reliable.
