Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Writo is in beta. This policy is a plain-language draft and will be reviewed by a lawyer, including the data localisation rules of the Republic of Kazakhstan, before paid plans launch.

The short version

  • We collect as little as possible.
  • Your essay is sent to an AI model to be checked. Without an account it is not stored. With an account, it is saved to your history unless you switch history off (at sign-up or any time later).
  • We never sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • You can download or delete your data at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password hash (a scrambled version that cannot be turned back into your password).
  • Settings you choose: whether history is on, and, only if you add them in your profile, a target band and exam date. We never ask for your date of birth.
  • Essays and results: while history is on. History is on by default for new accounts: the "Save my essays" box is ticked when you sign up, and you can untick it there or switch it off later in Privacy settings. If you checked an essay just before signing up with history on, that essay and its result are saved too.
  • Usage counters: to enforce daily limits we store a keyed hash of your IP address (not the address itself) together with a count, and delete it within 2 days.
  • Feedback: if you rate a check, we store your rating, your optional comment, the band shown and a keyed hash of your IP address (to stop spam). We do not link it to your account.
  • Waitlist: your email, if you ask to be notified about paid plans.
  • Analytics: anonymous page-view statistics from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies.

How your essay is processed

When you press Check, your essay and task are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI, an AI service run by Cloudflare, Inc., which returns the feedback. Writo is hosted on Cloudflare and its database is Cloudflare D1. Cloudflare may process data in different countries. We do not use your essays to train AI models.

Cookies and local storage

We use one essential cookie to keep you logged in. Your theme choice, your draft essay and, until you sign up, your last result are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Essays: until you delete them or delete your account. Switching history off stops new essays being saved; it does not delete old ones, so use Delete all my essays for that.
  • Usage counters: up to 2 days.
  • Feedback: up to 12 months.
  • Waitlist emails: until paid plans launch and we send the announcement, or until you ask us to remove you.

Your rights
