Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Writo is in beta. This policy is a plain-language draft and will be reviewed by a lawyer, including the data localisation rules of the Republic of Kazakhstan, before paid plans launch.

The short version

  • We collect as little as possible.
  • Your essay is sent to an AI model to be checked. Without an account it is not stored on our servers: your recent checks stay only in your browser. With an account, every essay you check is saved to your history, one copy of each essay. You can delete them at any time.
  • We never sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • You can download or delete your data at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password hash (a scrambled version that cannot be turned back into your password).
  • Settings you choose: only if you add them in your profile, a target band and exam date. We never ask for your date of birth.
  • Essays and results (account owners): every essay you check with your account and its result, so you can see your progress. If you check the same essay again, we keep one copy. Checks you made on the same device before signing up or logging in are moved to your account. You can delete all your essays or your whole account at any time in Privacy settings.
  • Usage counters: to enforce daily limits we store keyed hashes (not the original values) of your IP address and of a random device ID, together with a count, and delete them within 2 days.
  • Feedback, ideas and problem reports: if you rate a check or send us an idea or a problem, we store your rating or message, the page and band shown, and a keyed hash of your IP address (to stop spam). We do not link it to your account, so please do not include personal details in your message.
  • Waitlist and interest: your email, if you press "Notify me" to hear about paid plans. If you press "I'm interested" instead, we store one anonymous vote with a keyed hash of your IP address (to count one vote per network), no email.
  • Analytics: anonymous page-view statistics from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies.

How your essay is processed

When you press Check, your essay and task are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI, an AI service run by Cloudflare, Inc., which returns the feedback. Writo is hosted on Cloudflare and its database is Cloudflare D1. Cloudflare may process data in different countries. We do not use your essays to train AI models.

Cookies and local storage

We use two essential cookies: one keeps you logged in, the other holds a random device ID so that free checks are counted per device and not per network (it contains nothing about you and lasts one year). Your theme choice, your sound choice (sounds are off unless you turn them on), your draft essay and, without an account, your recent checks (up to 20) are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Essays: until you delete them (Delete all my essays) or delete your account.
  • Usage counters: up to 2 days.
  • Feedback: up to 12 months.
  • Waitlist emails: until paid plans launch and we send the announcement, or until you ask us to remove you.

Your rights
