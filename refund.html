Refund policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Simple version: if a paid plan doesn't help you, ask for your money back within 7 days and you will get it.

Paid plans are not available yet, so nobody is charged today. This policy will apply from the day they launch.

7-day refund, no questions

You can request a full refund within 7 days of any payment, including renewals. We don't ask why.

How to ask

Write to giantta02@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or reply to your payment receipt. Refunds go back to the original payment method through our payment partner, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Cancel in one click

You can cancel at any time from your account. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and you are never charged again after cancelling.

No surprise renewals

We email you before every renewal with the date and the amount, so you always have time to cancel.

Your text is safe

We never delete an essay to push you to pay. If a check fails for technical reasons, it does not count towards your limit.
